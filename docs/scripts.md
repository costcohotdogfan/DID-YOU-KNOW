# Scripts in the house voice

Rewritten 2026-11-22 to the register that worked for The Pool Route: the narrator says the saddest true thing in the book out loud, thrilled, and never slows down. Every line ends on an exclamation mark so the voice does not act the sadness. Each runs 150 to 180 words, about 45 to 55 seconds.

For narration, add an excited direction tag at the head of each paragraph, as in `episodes/01-pool-route/episode.md`.

Place names (Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia) are regions from Rockstar's own site. Check any street-level detail in the game before posting.

## The Escort Mission

Backbone: Of Mice and Men.

Did you know?! GTA VI has a hidden escort mission that 99% of players never finish!

Take the ranch job in Grassrivers with your partner! He has max strength and no fine motor control! Pro tip: don't let him pet anything! Mouse, puppy! It's a pattern!

Every night he asks you to tell him about the farm you're saving for! The little house! The rabbits! Check your objectives! It's not there! It was never a real objective! It's just the thing two guys say so they can get to sleep!

Here's the part most players miss: everyone on this ranch is alone! The old man, the stable hand, the boss's wife! Nobody even gave her a name! You two are the only ones who have somebody, and that's exactly what the game takes away!

Final mission: back to the river where you started! Tell him about the rabbits one more time! He's so happy! You know which button!

Reward: you get to be like everybody else now! Like and subscribe!
