# DID YOU KNOW?! — The Bureaucracy Scripts

The new lane, chosen 23 November: a tip that sounds real for five seconds, then turns into painfully specific paperwork. The goal is followers for Secret Third Thing, so nothing here needs you to have read a book.

## Rules of the lane

- The first line has to be believable. It hangs on something the game really has: stolen cars, a safehouse, a mailbox, money laundering, a phone.
- After that, every detail is exact: a ticket number, a form color, a closing time, a first name.
- The game's usual answers are switched off. No skip button, no weapons, and shooting the clerk makes it worse.
- The reward is small, late, or wrong.
- Same narrator as before: thrilled the whole way through, never notices it's miserable. Every line ends on an exclamation mark.
- Length is 190 to 220 words, about 55 to 65 seconds.

## The DMV

Did you know?! If you steal a hundred cars in GTA VI, the game mails you a letter!

Check the mailbox at your safehouse! Your license is suspended! You have to go to the DMV in Vice City! Weekdays only! Nine to four! Closed for lunch from eleven-thirty to one-fifteen!

Take a number! You're B47! They're serving B12! Pro tip: there's no skip button! Your weapon wheel is disabled! Your gun's in a little tray by the door! Sit down! The guy next to you is eating soup!

Ninety minutes later, they call B46! Then they switch to the C numbers! Nobody explains this!

You're up! Wrong form! You filled out the green one! You need the blue one! The blue one's at window six! Window six closed four minutes ago!

Come back tomorrow! New number! Now she needs proof of address! Your safehouse is in a dead guy's name! Bring a utility bill! It has to be an original! You printed yours! She can tell!

Day three! Everything's in order! The system's down!

Day four: the photo! You blink! That's the photo! No retakes!

Reward: a temporary paper license that expires in thirty days, and the real one's coming in the mail! It isn't! Like and subscribe!

## The Audit

Did you know?! If you launder more than two million dollars in GTA VI, you unlock a secret character: Gary from the IRS!

He knocks on your safehouse at 8 AM on a Tuesday! Don't run! Don't shoot! If you shoot Gary, they send two Garys! Offer him coffee! He'll say he's fine! Offer again!

Now the mini-game starts! He opens a laptop from 2009! He wants receipts for the car wash! You own a car wash! It has washed zero cars and made nine hundred thousand dollars!

Pro tip: do not say where the money came from! Every dialogue option says where the money came from! Pick "I'd have to check with my accountant!" Your accountant's a guy named Dre! Nobody's seen Dre since March! Don't say that either!

Gary asks about the boat! You have to prove the boat's a business expense! It has a hot tub!

Six in-game hours later, Gary closes the laptop! He says you'll hear from them in six to eight weeks! He takes a cookie! He leaves!

Reward: a letter in six to eight weeks! You owe four hundred dollars! You overpaid on the car wash! Like and subscribe!

## Jury Duty

Did you know?! There's one crime in GTA VI the cops will never forgive!

Check your mailbox! Under the pizza coupons! That's a jury summons! Ignore it and nothing happens! Ignore the second one and you get six stars! There's no six stars! They added one!

So go! County courthouse, 7:45 AM! Park in the juror lot! The juror lot's full! Park eleven blocks away!

Now you wait in a room with ninety NPCs and one vending machine! It takes exact change! Pro tip: you have four million dollars and no quarters!

They call your name at 2 PM! The lawyer asks if you've ever been the victim of a crime! Then he asks if you've ever committed one! Scroll down! The list is alphabetical! It takes eleven minutes!

You get picked anyway! The case is a guy who stole one car! One! You've stolen four hundred! You have to sit there and look disappointed in him!

The trial's nine days! No phone! Lunch isn't provided!

Reward: fifteen dollars a day and a certificate, and you're back in the pool in twelve months! Like and subscribe!

## The HOA

Did you know?! If you buy a house with a lawn in GTA VI, you automatically join the homeowners association!

Nobody tells you! You find out when a woman named Denise leaves a note on your door! Your trash cans are visible from the street! That's a fine!

Park a stolen helicopter on the lawn? Fine! Paint the house pink? You needed approval! The approved colors are Sand, Light Sand and Sandstone!

Pro tip: you can't get rid of Denise! Players have tried everything! She's back at the next meeting with a longer agenda!

So go to the meeting! First Thursday of the month, 7 PM, in the clubhouse! There's a forty-minute discussion about mulch! You can't skip it! You have to vote! If you abstain, they make you treasurer!

Now you're the treasurer! You have to open a spreadsheet! Inside GTA! Somebody spent six hundred dollars on a banner! Find out who! It was Denise!

Final mission: run against her for president! You lose! Forty-one to two! The other vote was a guy who thought you were someone else!

Reward: you can keep the pink, but you have to repaint the mailbox! Like and subscribe!

## The Day Job

The hidden-door one: go here at this time, and what's behind it is worse than a monster.

Did you know?! There's a door in GTA VI that only opens at 8:59 on a Monday morning!

Go to the waffle place on the highway! Men's room, third stall! Flush twice, then hold the handle! The mirror swings open! Go in!

It's an office! Carpet! Fluorescent lights! And here's the weird part: everyone knows you! A guy says "morning!" Your badge works! You have a desk! There's a photo of a dog on it! You don't have a dog!

Sit down! Log in! The password's on a sticky note! You have four thousand unread emails! Most of them are about the fridge!

Pro tip: at ten there's a mandatory training video about ladder safety! Twenty-two minutes! Can't skip! There's a quiz! You need eighty percent! There are no ladders in this office!

At noon there's cake for Linda! Sign the card! Everyone already wrote "Happy Birthday!" You have to think of something else!

Your manager wants to circle back on the Henderson thing! Nobody will tell you what the Henderson thing is!

At five the door unlocks! Check your bank account! You've been here eleven years! It's all direct deposit!

Reward: two weeks' vacation, and you have to get it approved! Like and subscribe!

## The Claim

Did you know?! You can file an insurance claim in GTA VI!

Next time you wreck a car you actually own, don't walk away! Open your phone! Call the number on the card in the glove box! You're on hold! The hold music's four minutes long and it loops! Your wait is forty minutes!

Pro tip: don't put the phone down! If you switch weapons, you lose your place in line!

A woman named Trish picks up! She needs your policy number! It's on the card! The card was in the car! The car's on fire!

Now describe the accident! Pick from the menu! There's no option for "I drove it off a parking garage on purpose!" Pick "weather!"

Trish sends an adjuster! He'll be there between eight and noon! Wait at the wreck! You can't leave! He comes at 3:40! He takes one photo! He says the car's worth nine hundred dollars! You paid two hundred thousand! He says, "Not according to the book!"

You can appeal! The appeal's a fax! Go find a fax machine!

Reward: a check for six hundred dollars, after the deductible, and your premium goes up! Like and subscribe!

## Automating the pipeline

What each step needs, most automatic first.

| Step | Who does it now | Can it be automatic? |
|---|---|---|
| Find the pauses in the narration | `tools/silences.sh` in the repo | Already is |
| Render the cut with the HUD and verify the file | `tools/build_cut.py` in the repo | Already is |
| Tag the script for the hyped read | By hand | Yes, a few lines of code |
| Generate two narration takes | Claude, through the ElevenLabs connector | Yes, with an ElevenLabs API key on BEAST |
| Draft the edit list, one cut per paragraph break | By hand | Mostly: cut on the longest pauses, then a person nudges |
| Captions | Not done yet | Yes, from the script and the pauses |
| Midjourney stills and motion | Claude or Grokbot, in the browser | No official API. It stays a browser job. |
| Approve the script, heart the picks, watch the final | Jamie | No, and it shouldn't be |

The brief for T3 Code, in one line: add a command that takes a script heading from this doc and produces the episode folder, the tagged narration text, two narration takes, and a draft edit list, so the only manual work left is Midjourney and picking.

The render has to run on BEAST, where the footage is.
