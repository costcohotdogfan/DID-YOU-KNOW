## Script (about 200 words)

Did you know?! There's a character in GTA VI who refuses to do crimes, because crimes are phony.

Get kicked out of the prep school upstate, put on the red hunting hat, and take the train to the city. Now press X on anyone to call them a phony. Press it twice for goddamn phony. That's the whole combat system!

The carjackers? Phonies. The cops? Goddamn phonies. The guy playing piano at the hotel bar? Phoniest guy in the game, and the crowd loves him, which just about kills your character. It really does.

Classic GTA: the elevator guy offers to send a girl up. Say yes! When she gets there, your character just wants to talk. He's too depressed. Pay her anyway. Then the elevator guy comes back for five more dollars and punches you in the stomach. No wanted stars.

Pro tip: check your stats. You've lied to every NPC you've met. You're the phoniest one here, and you sort of know it, if you want to know the truth.

Final mission: watch your kid sister on the carousel in the rain. She's the only thing in the city that isn't phony. Reward: you're so happy you cry. Like and subscribe!

For narration, apply the episode 01 treatment: an excited tag on every paragraph and exclamation marks on the sad lines.

## Shot list

It has to read as real footage. The joke is what the character does in the world. Three HUD touches at most: the mission card, a phony meter, the ennui bar.

1. **Arrival.** He walks out of the train station in the red hunting hat, suitcase in hand, into normal city chaos. Mission card: "DON'T GO HOME."
2. **The carjacking.** A carjacking is in progress. He walks up, points at the carjacker, says his piece, and keeps walking. The carjacker and the driver both watch him go.
3. **The payphone.** A police chase tears down the street behind him. He's in the foreground at a payphone, dialing, then hanging up before anyone answers.
4. **The piano bar.** The whole room is applauding the piano player. He's the one person sitting with his arms crossed. Phony meter in the corner, full.
5. **The hotel room.** He sits on the edge of the bed in his coat and hat. She stands by the door. He's just talking. Hold it too long.
6. **After the punch.** He's on the carpet. No respawn. The camera drifts in a slow idle orbit, like the player put the controller down.
7. **The pond.** Frozen pond, him alone on a bench, no ducks. The ennui bar fills, slowly.
8. **The carousel.** Rain. He's soaked on a bench, watching, smiling. The camera lifts away the way it does at the end of a mission.

Expect shot 5 to need covering as two singles: Midjourney would not animate a man and a woman together in episode 01.
